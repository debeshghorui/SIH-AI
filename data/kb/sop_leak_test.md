# SOP — Leak Test

## Purpose

A leak test confirms the integrity of a flange, valve, or instrument connection after isolation and reinstatement. It is mandatory before returning equipment to service.

## Scope

Applies to all flanged joints on hydrocarbon service, including the cooling water return manifold near 12-FT-208.

## Procedure

1. Pressurise the line to operating pressure with nitrogen.
2. Hold for 10 minutes; pressure drop must be less than 0.5%.
3. Apply soap solution to all flanges and observe for bubbles.
4. If a leak is found, depressurise, re-torque the flange, and repeat from step 1.
5. Record the test pressure, hold time, and result in the inspection log.

## Acceptance

- Zero visible bubbles at any joint.
- Pressure drop within tolerance.
- No audible hiss.

## Rejection

Any leak rejects the joint. Do not return to service. Raise a defect note and notify the shift supervisor.
