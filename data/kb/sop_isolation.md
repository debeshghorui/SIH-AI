# SOP — Equipment Isolation

## Purpose

This procedure governs the safe isolation of process equipment at MRPL before maintenance, inspection, or hot work. Isolation removes the equipment from service and ensures no hazardous energy can reach the worker.

## Scope

Applies to all tagged instruments and vessels in the crude inlet, slop, and cooling water lines, including transmitters 12-P-104, 12-FT-208, and 12-LT-310.

## Isolation steps

1. Notify the control room shift supervisor and obtain a Permit to Work (see SOP — Permit to Work).
2. Close the upstream and downstream block valves. Apply chain and lock.
3. Open the bleed valve and verify pressure is zero on the local gauge.
4. Drain the line to the slop tank; confirm liquid is zero.
5. Fit a spectacle blind or spade at the flange. Record the blind number.
6. Test the atmosphere for LEL, H2S, and O2 before personnel entry.
7. Tag the isolated equipment with a yellow isolation tag showing the date and the isolator's name.

## Reinstatement

1. Remove the isolation tag after sign-off.
2. Remove the spectacle blind.
3. Close the bleed valve.
4. Open the block valves slowly and check for leaks.
5. Inform the control room that the equipment is back in service.

## Safety

- Never isolate a running pump without switching to the standby.
- Bleed-down must reach zero before opening the flange.
- Two-person rule applies for all bleed and blind steps.
