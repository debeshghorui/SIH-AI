# SOP — Permit to Work

## Purpose

A Permit to Work (PTW) is the controlled authorisation that must be in place before any non-routine work is carried out on plant equipment. No PTW, no work.

## Scope

All maintenance, inspection, hot work, confined space entry, and electrical isolation at MRPL.

## Permit types

- **Cold Work Permit** — mechanical work without ignition sources.
- **Hot Work Permit** — welding, grinding, or any spark-producing work. Requires gas test.
- **Confined Space Entry** — requires atmosphere test and a stand-by person.

## Issue

1. The permit is issued by the Authorised Person (shift supervisor or above).
2. The permit lists the equipment tag, the work scope, the isolations in place, and the gas test result.
3. The permit is valid for one shift only and must be signed off at the end of the shift.

## Sign-off

1. Confirm the work is complete and the area is clean.
2. Confirm all isolations are removed and the equipment is ready for service.
3. The permit holder and the Authorised Person both sign the permit closure.

## Audit

Permits are audited monthly. A missing or unsigned permit is a high-priority finding.
